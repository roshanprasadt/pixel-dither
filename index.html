Pixel Dither

Any image, as dithered pixel art. Right in your browser.

Big square pixels, a handful of colours taken from the picture itself, and the regular dot patterns of classic pixel art where its gradients used to be.

    • Private

      Your image never leaves your device. There is no server; everything happens in this page.

    • Works offline

      Install it from your browser and it opens like an app, with or without a connection.

    • Open source

      Free under the MIT licence. Read it, fork it or use it as a library, on GitHub (opens in a new tab).

    How it works

    1. 1 Shrink

      The picture is scaled down to a grid of big pixels, averaging detail rather than skipping it.

    2. 2 Find the palette

      Clustering finds the few colours that best represent the picture, so the result keeps its mood.

    3. 3 Dither

      Each pixel is nudged by a Bayer pattern before taking its nearest colour, turning smooth gradients into checkerboards and dot fields.
